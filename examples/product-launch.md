# Product Launch Plan

The **Aurora 2** ships in *Q3* with a redesigned onboarding flow.

~ This is the launch plan for Aurora 2. The headline: it ships in the
third quarter with a completely redesigned onboarding flow.

---

## Highlights

- **3x faster** cold start
- New <span style="color:#2e86c1">dark theme</span>
- Offline sync — <u>finally</u>

~ Three headline features: cold start is three times faster, there is a
new dark theme, and offline sync finally ships.

---

## Rollout order

1. Internal dogfood
2. Beta cohort
3. General availability

~ Rollout happens in three waves. First internal dogfooding, then the
beta cohort, then general availability.

---

## Launch-day risk

Server capacity is the main risk.

Mitigation: staged traffic ramp + feature flags.

~ The main launch-day risk is server capacity. We mitigate it with a
staged traffic ramp and feature flags so we can dial features off
without a redeploy.

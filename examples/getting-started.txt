# Meet Slideshow Studio

Turn any outline into a narrated video.

Write slides as plain text, pick a voice,
and export an MP4 with natural speech.

~ Welcome to Slideshow Studio. This quick tour shows how a simple text
file becomes a fully narrated video.

---

## Draft in text

Type your deck in any editor.

A hash starts a slide, blank lines split
paragraphs, and tilde lines become narration.

~ Everything you see on this slide was written in a plain text file.
No layout tools needed for a first draft.

---

## Refine in the studio

Import, then polish.

Edit rich text, apply layout templates,
add Ken Burns motion and per-slide voices.

~ Once imported, the deck is fully editable. Change the wording, tune
the motion, or give a single slide its own voice and speed.

---

## Narrate with one click

Choose from hundreds of neural voices.

Edge-tts works online, Piper runs fully offline,
and the studio remembers your last voice.

~ Narration is generated automatically. Empty slides can borrow their
text from the slide body with one menu command.

---

## Export anywhere

MP4 video with burned-in or subtitle-file captions,
audio-only MP3 or WAV, or a standalone web page.

~ When you are happy, export to MP4 for video platforms, MP3 for a
podcast style release, or HTML to share in a browser. Thanks for
watching.
